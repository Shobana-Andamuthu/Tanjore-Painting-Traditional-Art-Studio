Our heritage

The art of Tanjore painting

For over four centuries, Thanjavur artists have created devotional paintings with a distinctive look: glowing gold, embossed jewellery and deep, jewel-toned colour. At Swarnam, every panel is made by hand in the traditional way.

  • Genuine 22-carat gold leaf applied by hand
  • Semi-precious stones set into raised gesso
  • Seasoned jackfruit and teak wood panels
  • Natural and mineral pigments for lasting colour

Our process

From sketch to gold

Five careful stages turn a blank board into a radiant heirloom.

1

Sketch

The master draws the composition on cloth-covered wood.

2

Gesso relief

Chalk paste is built up for ornaments and jewellery.

3

Gold leaf

Pure gold sheets are laid on the dried relief.

4

Gem setting

Rubies, emeralds and pearls are set by hand.

5

Colour & finish

Rich pigments, outlines and a final polish.
