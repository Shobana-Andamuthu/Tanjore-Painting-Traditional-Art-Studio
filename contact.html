FAQ

Quick answers

How long does a commission take?

Small paintings take two to three weeks, medium works four to six weeks and large panels eight to twelve weeks. Festival deadlines can be planned in advance.

Can I request changes while it is being painted?

Absolutely. We share a pencil sketch for approval and progress photos at the gesso, gold and colour stages, so you can request refinements early.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes. Paintings are packed in custom wooden crates with insured courier delivery to more than 14 countries.

Is the gold leaf genuine?

Yes. We use 22-carat gold leaf and every artwork ships with a certificate of authenticity signed by the master artist.
